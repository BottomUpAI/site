Visual directions

Same content in each: your name, link labels, and [[WRITE: …]] markers where your prose goes. Each direction is shown in light and dark (the site follows the system setting). The marker style is shared and never ships; it's here so you can see it reads as unmistakably unfinished in every direction. The live pieces will carry the color; the frame stays quiet.

A. Journal

Source Serif 4 for reading, Inter for navigation and metadata. Warm off-white and near-black, a deep blue for links. Reads like a well-set academic journal or a long-form research blog.

B. Lab notebook

IBM Plex Sans throughout, Plex Mono for navigation, dates and metadata. Cool grays and a teal link color. Technical and exact; closest to an engineering-research feel.

C. Monograph

A single serif family (Newsreader) for everything, larger name, pure black and white, links distinguished only by underline. The most austere; everything visual is left to the pieces.
